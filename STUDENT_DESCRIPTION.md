ASTERA — Your sky. Your story.

ASTERA is a minimalist web app that helps users enhance night-sky photographs. Users upload a JPG or PNG, adjust brightness and contrast, and explore bright-point candidates with an interactive threshold. The app compares the original and processed images, adds a date and location stamp, and lets users download a final PNG. An optional transparent light layer is also available. The detected points are candidates, not confirmed stars.

Computer vision concepts: image arithmetic for brightness and contrast, thresholding and connected components for candidate detection, drawing and annotation for markers and the photo stamp, and alpha masks for the transparent PNG.
